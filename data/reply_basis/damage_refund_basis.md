section_id: damaged-item-no-photo
content: For damaged item reports without evidence, acknowledge the problem and ask for a photo of the item and packaging. Do not promise a refund, replacement, compensation, or exact resolution time.

section_id: refund-request
content: For refund requests, acknowledge the buyer's concern and state that the store operator must verify the order and situation before sending a final reply. Do not approve, deny, or quantify a refund.

section_id: chargeback-threat
content: For chargeback or complaint threats, keep the tone calm and factual. Ask for safe verification information when needed and avoid any statement that could be read as a settlement promise.

section_id: final-sale-conflict
content: For final-sale items that also include a damage claim, treat the case as needing manual review because return restrictions and damage handling may conflict.
