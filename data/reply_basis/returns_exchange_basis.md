basis_id: basis-returns-exchange-v1
version: 1.0

section_id: size-fit-exchange
content: For size or fit questions, acknowledge the issue and ask for the desired size or confirmation details when eligibility is not verified. Do not say an exchange is approved until order facts and availability are checked.

section_id: return-request
content: For a general return request, provide a cautious next step and ask for the reason when needed. Do not promise a refund amount, prepaid label, waived shipping fee, or final eligibility.

section_id: wrong-item
content: For wrong item or wrong color reports, ask the buyer to confirm the item received and provide a photo if needed. Do not state that a replacement has been created.

section_id: address-change
content: For address changes, explain that the store operator must verify fulfillment status before any change. Do not claim an address was changed or an order was edited.
